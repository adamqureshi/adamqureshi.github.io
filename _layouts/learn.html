Before you book

A few fair questions.

Is this $750 per hour?+

No. The pilot costs $750 for the complete package: a 90-minute workshop, a 30-minute follow-up within 14 days, and your campaign plan and checklist. Advertising spend is additional and paid through your own ad account.

Do I need an approved advertising account?+

For a hands-on campaign build, you need access to the relevant Ads Manager features. We check your account status before confirming the session. If access or approval is pending, we agree whether a planning session is useful or wait until you’re ready.

Will my ad be live at the end?+

That depends on account readiness, billing, platform review, and your approval. Our goal is a campaign draft and clear next steps. The workshop does not guarantee approval, same-day delivery, leads, or sales.

Do I share my password or card details with you?+

No. You log in yourself and control the account. Pause screen sharing whenever you enter passwords, payment information, or other private details.

Do I need special course software?+

No. We use a live video call with screen sharing. Bring a laptop or desktop, a reliable connection, and access to your business website and ad account. The call link and preparation list come by email.

Does this include a website or tracking integration?+

We review your landing page and discuss a measurement plan using the features available in your account. Building a website, writing custom tracking code, or creating integrations is outside the workshop and can be quoted separately.

What if I’d rather have you run the ads?+

Choose the managed service: $500 setup plus $500 per month, with ad spend separate. Coaching helps you run your own campaigns; management covers ongoing work by me.
