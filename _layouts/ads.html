A few fair questions

Before we start.

How much should I spend on ads?+

We’ll agree a testing budget based on your offer, market, and what you can comfortably spend. Advertising is billed separately from my service fee. You approve the budget before launch and before any increase.

Can you guarantee leads or sales?+

No. This is a paid test with a clear goal and regular reviews. We’ll look at the available data and your actual customer outcomes. Results depend on your offer, market, budget, website, and follow-up.

Does paying for ads get me recommended by ChatGPT?+

This service manages paid advertising. It does not promise inclusion in ChatGPT’s answers, organic rankings, or an endorsement from OpenAI.

What do you need from me?+

Your website, the product or service you want to promote, your service area, and an idea of your budget. We’ll check advertiser access and approvals first, then agree the scope. You keep ownership of your account.

What happens after the pilot?+

We review the results together and agree whether to continue month to month, change the test, or stop. If the data is still too limited, I’ll tell you what we can and can’t conclude.
