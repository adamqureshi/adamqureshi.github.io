A few fair questions

Before we start.

Can you help me launch a competitor?+

Yes. We start with a specific customer and a reason they would choose your product. We develop original work using your skills and experience; we don’t need your former employer’s code, private data, or confidential materials.

Does the launch plan include building the product?+

The first engagement covers the brief, MVP scope, customer test plan, and next-step estimate above. If development makes sense, we agree on deliverables, responsibilities, timing, and fees separately.

What if I’m still figuring out my idea?+

Tell me what you know well and what people struggle with in that industry. We can start there. You don’t need to arrive with a finished product specification.

How much does it cost?+

It depends on the scope. Send me a short overview and your budget range if you have one. I’ll propose a clear fee and deliverables before you commit. Any software or development costs are discussed separately.
