## Who this is for

- You have steady income but you want a simpler investing plan
- You prefer a long-term approach (not trading)
- You want accountability and clear next steps

## What I am building (simple, human-first)

- A quick intake to understand goals and timeline
- A basic risk profile (how much market ups/downs you can realistically tolerate)
- A straightforward index approach (diversified, long-term, rules-based)
- Automation: invest monthly without thinking about it
- Check-ins to keep the plan on track

## What I do not do

- No day trading
- No hype, no promises, no "guaranteed" returns
- No custody of your money (your funds stay in your own account)

## How I use AI (without the hype)

I use AI tools to speed up onboarding, summarize goals, and explain tradeoffs in plain English.
AI is used as decision support -- it does not "predict markets," and final decisions are made by people.

## A quick note on risk

All investing involves risk. Even diversified index funds can decline in value. Nothing on this site is a promise or guarantee.
